# Umbrajax — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 2026**

Umbrajax is developed by **Ploovius**. This policy explains what data the game does and does not handle.

## 1. What we collect

**Nothing on our servers.** We operate no game servers and no player accounts. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to log into.

## 2. Data stored on your device

Your progress, settings, and purchase entitlements are stored **only on your own device**, in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device and we cannot see or access it.

- Clearing your browser data or switching devices will erase your progress and purchases. We cannot restore them.

## 3. Payments

In-game purchases are processed by **Stripe** via Stripe-hosted checkout pages. Your payment details go directly to Stripe and are governed by [Stripe's privacy policy](https://stripe.com/privacy). We receive only a confirmation that the payment completed — never your card number or payment credentials.

## 4. What we do NOT collect

- No accounts, names, or email addresses
- No tracking, analytics, or telemetry
- No advertising identifiers
- No location data

## 5. Children's privacy

Umbrajax is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Because we collect no personal information at all, there is nothing to delete — but a parent or guardian may contact us with any concern.

## 6. Data retention

Since we hold no personal data on any server, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Data on your device remains there until you clear it.

## 7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted with the game. Continued play after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.

## 8. Contact

For any privacy question or request: **pluviandev@outlook.com**
