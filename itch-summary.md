# Umbrajax — Legal Summary (for itch.io page)

Umbrajax is a free browser arcade game by **Ploovius**. No accounts, no login.

**Privacy:** We collect nothing. There are no game servers, no analytics, and no tracking. Your progress and settings are stored only on your own device (browser localStorage). In-game purchases are processed by **Stripe** through Stripe-hosted checkout — your payment details go directly to Stripe under its own privacy policy; we never see or store them.

**Terms:** You get a personal, non-transferable license to play. Virtual goods (Void Cores, skins, boosters) are licensed, not sold — they have no cash value, can't be transferred, and live in your device's save data, so clearing browser data deletes them permanently. No cheating, exploiting, or abusing the payment/redeem systems. The game is provided as-is, without warranties.

**Refunds:** Virtual goods are delivered instantly via redeem codes, so all sales are final except where required by law (e.g. EU withdrawal rights apply only before content is redeemed or consumed). Duplicate charges or missing codes will always be fixed.
