# Umbrajax — Refund Policy

**Last updated: October 2026**

Umbrajax is developed by **Ploovius**. All purchases are in US dollars (USD) and processed by Stripe.

## 1. General rule

Virtual goods (Void Cores, skins, boosters, bundles) are **delivered instantly** when you redeem your code in the game's Store. Because digital delivery is immediate and cannot be returned, **all sales are final and non-refundable**, except where required by law.

## 2. Exceptions required by law

Some jurisdictions grant a right of withdrawal for digital purchases (for example, the EU 14-day right for digital content). Where such a right applies:

- it applies only if you have **not yet redeemed or consumed** the purchased content;
- redeeming a code or spending Void Cores counts as performance of the contract having begun, which ends the withdrawal right.

If you believe you qualify, contact us within the applicable period.

## 3. How to request a refund

Email **pluviandev@outlook.com** with:

- the email or name used at checkout (if any),
- the item purchased and the date,
- your redeem code or order details from Stripe.

We review requests case by case and respond within a reasonable time.

## 4. Duplicate charges and errors

If you were charged twice for the same item or charged without receiving a redeem code, contact us at **pluviandev@outlook.com** and we will fix it — either by delivering the missing items or refunding the duplicate charge.

## 5. Chargebacks and fraud

Filing a chargeback instead of contacting us, or obtaining items through fraud, abuse of the redeem system, or stolen payment methods, will result in the associated entitlements being revoked.

## 6. Contact

Refund questions: **pluviandev@outlook.com**
